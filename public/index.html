Field Transmission · 3:00 AM, The Lab "Tonight, We Make Them Listen" +

The lab is dark, only the backlights on. Rain paces slow, hat glowing faint electric blue. Trip sits cross-legged on the floor with the bass, sniffing his own fingers like inspiration has a smell and he just found it.

Rain stops. Turns to the camera. Points, dead certain: "Tonight is the night we make them listen."

Trip looks up, deadly serious: "They don't deserve us. We gotta dumb it down." Rain isn't moved: "No. We go raw. No effects. Just us and the wreckage."

They freeze. Long stare. Then, together: "What are we gonna do tonight? The same thing we do every night."

Field Transmission · Rooftop of an Abandoned Hardee's The Downpour +

3:00 AM. Rain stands on the rusted roof, black umbrella in hand, city lights melting off the edge like wax. Not a cloud in the sky — but the air feels wet anyway.

Trip climbs the wall using only his teeth, dragging a busted sub that leaks bass like blood. "Our bass is ready," he growls, sniffing the rooftop like a rabid mouse.

Rain opens the umbrella. It doesn't stop expanding — it becomes a dome, a cathedral of carbon fiber and lasers. Every rat on the block becomes a backing vocalist. Somewhere below, a drive-thru menu turns into a waveform.

Trip looks at Rain. Rain looks at Trip. "What are we gonna do?"
